Put a till in training mode  Put a till in training mode  This topic is primarily for administrators and other people who manage a Fiftytwo solution You may need to have particular user rights in order to use this functionality. Ask your Fiftytwo administrator if you're in doubt. When you onboard new shop assistants who must learn how to work at a till, you can put one or more of your tills in training mode, so that the new shop assistants can practice before they begin to serve real customers. Training mode is also useful if you need to test new setups. When a till is in training mode:  Transactions on the till aren't registered as real ones, so they're not sent to your organization's finance system  Receipts will prominently display the wording TRAINING, so that they can't be confused with real receipts  The till will have this symbol on its display to indicate that it's in training mode:  To put a till in training mode: 1. Expand Store maintenance 3. Select Terminal configuration 3. Find the required till:  In the filters section at the top of the Terminal configuration page, search  for the till's number or name. 4. In the row that represents the required till, click the till number. 5. On the General tab, in the Settings section, in the Training field, select 9 - Always. Always doesn't mean that the till will remain in training mode forever. It simply means that while the till is in training mode, it can only be used for training. You can also select one of the settings that let the till be used for training if a particular key is set. However, it's often safer to select the Always option because then there's no doubt about whether the till is in training mode or not. If you do select one of the settings that let the till be used for training if a particular key is set, it means that the required key must be set when the trainee logs in to the till and when the trainee logs out of the till. To apply your changes on the till, click Update master data in the top right corner or the page.  This will send all master data updates, that are available on the store controller and relevant to the till in question, to the till. This may include other master data than you've defined in 52ViKING Store Management's till configuration. Remember to put the till back in normal mode after training or testing has been completed. If you don't, you risk that real sales on the till aren't registered.
