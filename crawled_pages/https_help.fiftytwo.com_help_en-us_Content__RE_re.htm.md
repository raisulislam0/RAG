Background information about 52ViKING  52ViKING   previously also known as 52RETAIL is a fast and stable POS solution. 52ViKING is widely used, with more than three million transactions seamlessly going through the system every day. The solution is built with a modern tech stack that compiles data and makes the translation to machine code up to 50 percent faster than other programming languages, leading to a low average scan time. You can use 52ViKING for both smaller stores with few checkout terminals and a single pc workspace at the back of the store as well as for medium-sized stores, and for large stores with many checkout terminals and multiple pc workspaces in the office, receiving goods, stockroom, etc. The system's all-round and modular functionality supports the needs of many segments, for example:  The groceries segment, from mini-markets to supermarkets and hypermarkets  The builders merchant and specialist goods market, from smaller retail chains to large department store chains Fiftytwo has developed 52ViKING in collaboration with leading retail companies. 52ViKING is widely used in more than 4700 stores with around 16000 checkout terminals in grocery and specialist stores in Denmark, Iceland, Norway, Sweden, Germany, Poland, and more countries. Every week, more than 33 million consumers are handed a 52ViKING till receipt.  overview  has a wide range of features, which cover counter sales, payment, and customer service as well as back-store, retail chain management, and communication functions. Because 52ViKING is modular, you can set it up to match your organization's exact needs, from the independent store with a need for in-store features, to the centrally-managed retail chain that needs a checkout terminal system that's efficiently integrated with central product management and finance systems. For retail chains, the 52ViKING chain module offers an efficient interface between the chain's central systems and the store network. The 52ViKING chain module thus takes care of all distribution and collection of data to and from the 52ViKING checkout terminal and store systems in the chain's stores. Naturally, this distribution and collection can take place both with fixed batch runs or on an ongoing basis in a real-time online environment. For shopping centers, a special 52ViKING shopping center module ensures efficiency. A central server supports the joint central administrative functions, including any account and credit authorization systems. Each store runs their own business, managing their separate product and price indexes, balances, reports, and communication to their individual chain offices. If required, you can integrate other checkout and store systems into the 52ViKING solution and handle collection of sales information and management of account and credit management on a joint central basis. 52ViKING is easy to integrate with other systems because of its modular setup and its flexible importexport tool. This applies to finance, product management, logistics, electronic payment, external credit authorization and banking systems, CRM solutions, spreadsheets, internet commerce, electronic signage, electronic weighing, space management, and much more. 52ViKING is based on standard technology, which provides optimal reliability, quick and simple servicing, and not least continuous access to important information on product sales and markups for individual products, product groups, departments, stores, or even an entire chain.
