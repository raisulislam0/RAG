Examples:20  The article will cost 20 less.220  If the customer buys two articles, the purchase will cost 20 less.  X for Y discount Type x between two numbers to specify an X for Y discount. The first number specifies how many articles the customer must buy to trigger the discount. The second number specifies how many articles' price the purchase will cost. Example using EUR as currency: 4x3  The customer will be able to buy four articles for the price of three articles. Thus, if a single article costs EUR 10, the customer will be able to buy four articles for EUR 30, so the customer will be able to save EUR 10 compared with the normal price of EUR 40 for four articles.
