View groups PLU buttons  Set up view groups PLU buttons  This topic is primarily for administrators and other people who manage a Fiftytwo solution In  previously also known as 52RETAIL, view groups are used to display articles in a grid view in which  can tap required articles to add them to the journal and sell them. You typically use view groups also known as PLU buttons for articles that don't have barcodes, such as bread, fruit, and vegetables. However, hardware and DIY stores, garden centers, etc. sometimes also use view groups because some of their articles can be so bulky that they're difficult to scan, even though they do have barcodes. Also, many quick service restaurants use view groups on customer-facing self-service displays. The buttons in a view group are often grouped by article type, and may contain several levels of groups and subgroups.
